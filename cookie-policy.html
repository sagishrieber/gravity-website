Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how growgravity.com, operated by Gravity Linked Ltd., uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (like local storage) work the same way. They let a site remember your actions and preferences and, where enabled, measure how the site is used.

Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to work and to remember your choices (language, theme, accessibility settings, and your cookie-consent choice). Always active.
  • Analytics — help us understand usage so we can improve the site. Loaded only if you allow them.
  • Marketing — used to measure and personalize campaigns. Loaded only if you allow them.

Managing your choices

You can change your consent at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may then not work as intended.
