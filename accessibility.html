Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Gravity, operated by Gravity Linked Ltd. (Company No. 517043261), sees great importance in making its website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe every person has the right to use the web with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence, and we invest ongoing effort to make this site usable by the widest possible audience.

Level of accessibility on this site

This website is being made accessible in accordance with Israeli Standard 5568 ("Web Content Accessibility Guidelines"), which is based on the international WCAG 2.0 guidelines at Level AA, and in line with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773–2013.

Accessibility features on the site

  • A dedicated accessibility menu (the icon at the corner of every page) offering: text enlargement, high contrast, color inversion, grayscale, a light background, link highlighting and underlining, a readable font, heading highlighting, a larger cursor, and an option to stop animations.
  • Keyboard operability of the accessibility menu, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Respect for the operating-system "reduce motion" preference.
  • A responsive layout that works on mobile phones and tablets.
  • Your chosen settings are saved in your browser and persist across pages.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully adapted — in particular certain animations, embedded third-party content (such as scheduling widgets), and documents. We continue to audit and improve the site's accessibility over time. If you encounter an accessibility problem, please tell us using the details below and we will do our best to fix it.
